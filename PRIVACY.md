# FormFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

FormFlow is a Chrome extension that fills Google Forms from answers you save.

**What it stores:** the question and answer pairs you add or import, and your settings (date format, outline and learn-mode toggles). This data is kept only in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`.

**What it does not do:** FormFlow has no server. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data, uses no analytics or tracking, and makes no network requests of its own.

**Where it runs:** only on Google Forms pages (`docs.google.com/forms/*`). It reads the questions on the page to find a matching saved answer and writes your saved answer into the field. It never submits a form for you.

**Permissions:** `storage` keeps your saved answers; `activeTab` lets the popup tell the open form to refill; host access to `docs.google.com` lets the content script run on Google Forms.

**Optional support links:** the popup has a Support panel with a Buy Me a Coffee link and a UPI QR code. Clicking the link opens an external page. FormFlow never sees or stores any payment information.

**Your control:** you can edit or delete any saved answer in the popup, or remove the extension to delete everything.
